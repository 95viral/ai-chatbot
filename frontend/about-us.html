Our Mission

At AI Chatbot, we are committed to democratizing artificial intelligence technology and making advanced AI solutions accessible to everyone. Who We Are

We are a dedicated team of software engineers, AI researchers, and product specialists based in India. Our team combines years of experience in machine learning, natural language processing, and web development to create cutting-edge conversational AI solutions.

Our diverse team is passionate about pushing the boundaries of what's possible with artificial intelligence while maintaining a strong commitment to security, privacy, and user satisfaction.

What We Do

AI Chatbot provides intelligent conversational solutions powered by advanced AI technology. Our platform offers:

  • Smart Conversations: Natural language processing that understands context and provides meaningful responses
  • Document Intelligence: Analyze and extract insights from documents using advanced AI algorithms

  • Secure Authentication: OTP-based verification for maximum security

Technology Stack

Our platform is built using modern, scalable technologies:

  • Backend: Python with Flask framework for robust API development
